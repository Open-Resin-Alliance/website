Three of us were at 3DPrintopia on 28 and 29 September with two printers on the stand: the Prometheus mSLA and the Prometheus Mini. Sam, Ada and Paul were the ones there. Everything on that stand is published under TheContrappostoShop, the name the printers and the software come from.

## The two machines

The Prometheus mSLA is the open-source resin printer Odyssey was written for: the engine's own description says it is designed for the Apollo series of control boards and the Prometheus mSLA, and Orion drives Odyssey from a single-board computer. The Prometheus Mini came along with it.

## The name on the stand

TheContrappostoShop is where these projects are published, and it is the name the booth carried. It is not the last word on them, though: an organisation was set up in August to take these projects on and give them a home of their own, and it gets a proper introduction in the next post.

## Get involved

The printers are the hardware end of the same work the repositories hold: Odyssey and Orion are public, and so is the rest of what TheContrappostoShop publishes. The community Discord at [discord.gg/beFeTaPH6v](https://discord.gg/beFeTaPH6v) is where the printer and file-format conversations happen, and the repositories are at [github.com/TheContrappostoShop](https://github.com/TheContrappostoShop).
