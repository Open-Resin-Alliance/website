Three of us were at 3DPrintopia on 28 and 29 September with two printers on the stand: the Prometheus mSLA and the Prometheus Mini. Sam, Ada and Paul were the ones there. The work is still published under TheContrappostoShop, the name the printers and the software come from. The Open Resin Alliance already exists as an organisation, but it took this weekend to get it going: the projects that started under that shop are the ones moving across to it.

## The two machines

The Prometheus mSLA is the open-source resin printer that Odyssey was written for: the repository describes the engine as designed for the Apollo series of control boards and the Prometheus mSLA, and Orion drives Odyssey from a single-board computer. The Prometheus Mini came along with it.

## The name on the stand

TheContrappostoShop is where these projects are published, and it is the name the booth carried. The organisation's own profile puts the change plainly: some of these projects have grown and moved from TheContrappostoShop to the ORA, to better serve the community at large.

## Get involved

The printers are the hardware end of the same work the repositories hold: Odyssey and Orion are public, and so is everything else in the organisation. The community Discord at [discord.gg/beFeTaPH6v](https://discord.gg/beFeTaPH6v) is where the printer and file-format conversations happen, and the repositories are at [github.com/Open-Resin-Alliance](https://github.com/Open-Resin-Alliance).
