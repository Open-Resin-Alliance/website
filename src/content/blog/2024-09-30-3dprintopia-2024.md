There were three of us at 3DPrintopia on 28 and 29 September, and we brought two printers: the Prometheus mSLA and the Prometheus Mini. Sam, Ada and Paul. That was the whole team - no company behind it, just the three of us and a stand under the name TheContrappostoShop, which is where the printers and the software lived at the time.

## What we brought

The Prometheus mSLA is the machine the Odyssey engine was written for. Odyssey runs on the Apollo control board, Orion drives it from a single-board computer, and the mSLA is the printer both of them were built around. The Mini came along as the smaller of the two.

## The neighbours, and the poutine

We shared the stand with [DLLPDF](https://www.dllpdf.com/), a family-run hi-tech hobby shop from Maryland who sell 3D printer parts. Good people to spend two days next to, and worth a look if you are hunting for printer hardware.

The part of the weekend we still tell people about is dinner. We had an AC heated bed with us, and at some point it stopped being a printer part and became a hotplate: the gravy went on it, and we made poutine on the show floor. It worked.

## The name on the stand

TheContrappostoShop is the name the booth carried, because it was the name the projects were published under. We had spent August putting something together to give them a home of their own, and it gets a proper introduction in the next post.

## Get involved

The printers are the hardware end of the same work the repositories hold: Odyssey and Orion are public, and so is the rest of what TheContrappostoShop publishes. The community Discord at [discord.gg/beFeTaPH6v](https://discord.gg/beFeTaPH6v) is where the printer and file-format conversations happen, and the repositories are at [github.com/TheContrappostoShop](https://github.com/TheContrappostoShop).
