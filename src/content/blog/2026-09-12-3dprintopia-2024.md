Three of us were at 3DPrintopia on 28 and 29 September 2024 with two printers on the stand: the Prometheus mSLA and the Prometheus Mini. Sam, Ada and Paul were the ones there. The work was still published under TheContrappostoShop at that point, six weeks after the alliance was founded and some time before the projects it now hosts had moved across.

## The two machines

The Prometheus mSLA is the open-source resin printer that Odyssey was written for: the repository describes the engine as designed for the Apollo series of control boards and the Prometheus mSLA, and Orion drives Odyssey from a single-board computer. Both are hosted here now, and both began life under the same name that was on the stand that weekend. The Prometheus Mini came along with it.

## The name on the stand

TheContrappostoShop is where the projects that predate this organisation were published, and it is the name the booth carried in 2024. The organisation's own profile puts the change plainly: some of these projects have grown and moved from TheContrappostoShop to the ORA, to better serve the community at large. What was on display at 3DPrintopia is the earlier half of that sentence.

## Get involved

The printers are the hardware end of the same work the repositories hold: Odyssey and Orion are public, and so is everything else in the organisation. The community Discord at [discord.gg/beFeTaPH6v](https://discord.gg/beFeTaPH6v) is where the printer and file-format conversations happen, and the repositories are at [github.com/Open-Resin-Alliance](https://github.com/Open-Resin-Alliance).
